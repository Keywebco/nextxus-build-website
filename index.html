Service

We build sovereign websites

A website you own, that every search engine and AI can read, hosted free, with no tracking. Built once and built right.

What a sovereign website is

It is a website you own outright. It isn't locked into a platform that can raise prices, change the rules or switch you off. The code sits in your own repository, and the pages are plain HTML that every search engine and AI can read. Free hosting is the default. If one host disappears, the site moves to the next one in minutes.

Built for the thinnest of pocketbooks. A site built for scarcity scales up cleanly.

What is included

  • Readable by every machine

    Pre-rendered semantic HTML. Every word can be read without JavaScript, so Google, Bing and AI crawlers see all of it.

  • Free hosting, your name on it

    Hosted on GitHub Pages or another free host, under your own account and your own domain if you have one.

  • Fast, accessible design

    High contrast, large type and a screen-reader-friendly layout. Light motion that doesn't slow anything down.

  • Every button works

    Every link and button is checked live before handover. No dead ends.

  • Sales wired in

    Buy buttons connected to Gumroad, JIM or PayPal, so the site can take orders on day one.

  • No tracking

    No cookies, no ad pixels, no data harvesting. Your visitors stay your visitors.

How it works

  1. Tell us what it's for. One email describing your business, your audience and what the site should do.
  2. We audit what you have. Existing pages, logos and listings get reused rather than rebuilt.
  3. We build it in one pass. Every page is finished, with nothing left as a stub.
  4. We verify it live. Every page and link is checked on the real web address before we call it done.
  5. You own it. The repository, the files and the domain are yours.

See it in practice: the Federation Portal, nextxus.online and the Tools Directory are all built this way.
